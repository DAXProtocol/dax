Problem · Counterparty Risk in Informal Settlements

Stablecoin Adoption is Growing, but
Settlement Counterparty Risk Remains

Stablecoins (USDT & USDC) are increasingly used for cross-border freelance work, merchant trade, and informal currency swaps in Africa. However, participants face critical settlement risks:

⚠️
Centralized P2P Friction

Account freezes, arbitrary dispute rulings, and sudden withdrawal restrictions on centralized exchanges leave merchants and users vulnerable to unexpected loss of funds.

💬
Informal OTC Default Risk

Transactions arranged through WhatsApp or Telegram lack escrow protections. One party must always send fiat or crypto first, leading to recurring scams and non-delivery.

🏛️
Lack of Low-Cost Arbitration

Cross-border commercial dispute amounts ($50–$2,000) are too small for formal legal recourse, leaving merchants and freelancers with no accessible way to resolve disagreements.

Protocol Flow · 4-Stage Settlement Lifecycle

CREATE · ESCROW · EVIDENCE · RESOLVE

A non-custodial smart contract workflow running on Arbitrum One:

STAGE 01
CREATE
Parties define agreement terms, milestone deliverables, and deadlines, bound cryptographically with EIP-712 typed structured signatures.
STAGE 02
ESCROW
Tokens (USDC, USDT, ARB, ETH) are locked in `DAX_AgreementUpgradeable.sol`. Funds remain on-chain; neither DAX nor a third party holds custody.
STAGE 03
EVIDENCE
Deliverables, source code, or receipts are fingerprinted client-side with SHA-256 and committed as immutable Merkle roots on-chain.
STAGE 04
RESOLVE
Disputed escrows automatically escalate to `DAX_Court.sol`. Staked community jurors vote using a commit-reveal scheme with economic penalties for dishonesty.
Security Design · Client Protection & Efficiency

Practical Security &
Mechanism Design

Core architectural choices focused on user key safety, dispute integrity, and low transaction costs:

🛡️
Client-Side Key Vault

Keys generated via BIP-39/44 and stored in Android Keystore AES-GCM. Hardware biometric authentication (`BIOMETRIC_STRONG`) with 0 server-side key exposure.

🔒
Runtime Screen Privacy

Runtime `FLAG_SECURE` prevents OS task-switcher previews and blocks screenshots whenever private keys, seed phrases, or sensitive agreements are visible.

⚖️
Commit-Reveal Court

Two-phase commit-reveal voting in `DAX_Court.sol` (`keccak256(vote + salt)`) prevents vote-buying and bandwagoning. Malicious jurors forfeit staked tokens.

⚡
Arbitrum One Settlement

Optimized for Arbitrum One with sub-cent gas fees and fast L2 finality, making small-ticket escrows ($20–$500) practical and cost-effective.
